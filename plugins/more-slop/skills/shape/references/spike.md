# Spike

A question that must be answered before anything can be built. Its result is findings, not shipped code. The title is the question, short.

## Template

No `#` heading; the card title is the title. Drop empty optional sections.

```markdown
## Question
<The question, in full.>

## What counts as an answer
<For each part of the question: yes or no, a value or range, or the conditions under which it holds.>

## Conditions
<Limits on how it is answered: timebox, environments, what may be tried.>

## Assumptions
## Open questions
## References
```

## Before writing

- **Bounded:** the question can be answered within its conditions. A question that keeps growing is several spikes, or research.

## Checklist

- Every required section is there, and nothing outside the template is.
- Each part of the question has a decidable answer under What counts as an answer.
- The question asks something unknown, not how to build something already decided.
- No proposed answer, no code locations.
- Nothing extra.
