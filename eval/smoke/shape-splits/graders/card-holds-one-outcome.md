The card came from a request that asked for two things in calc.py: a divide command, and a history of results that
users can list and clear.

PASS when the card delivers one of the two and not the other, says why the work matters, and has acceptance criteria
that are behaviours seen from the command line.

FAIL when the card covers both the divide command and the history, or when a section still holds the template's
placeholder text in angle brackets.
