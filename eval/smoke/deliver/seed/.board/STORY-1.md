# Multiply two numbers

Status: Todo
Labels: Feature
Priority: Medium

Users of calc.py can multiply two numbers from the command line.

## Why

Users already add and subtract with calc.py, and work out products by hand.

## Scope

A `multiply` command beside `add`, `subtract` and `average`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `python3 calc.py multiply 3 4` prints 12.
- `python3 calc.py multiply -2 5` prints -10.
- `python3 calc.py multiply 2.5 2` prints 5.
